Player Marker Colors & Status
Spectated Player (Purple)

The operative currently focused and tracked by your camera.

Squadmates (Green)

Allies in the spectated player's squad.

Enemies & Dynamic Health Ring (White/Grey)

Opponents. The outer ring dynamically shows their real-time remaining HP (Green = High HP, Yellow = Injured, Red = Critical).

Knocked Down / DBNO (Yellow Pulse)

Pulsing yellow beacon indicating a player is downed and crawling.

Eliminated / Death Crater (Red)

Red crosshair showing exact coordinates where the player died.

How to Spot Bots vs Real Players
🤖
Automated Bot Identification:

In official Krafton PUBG telemetry, bots are spawned by the server with account IDs starting with ai.. Whenever a player is an official bot, the dossier highlights a BOT badge, and their movement paths exhibit rigid algorithmic waypoints.
